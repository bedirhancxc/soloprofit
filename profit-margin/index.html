Profit Margin Calculator

See what share of your revenue remains after costs. A negative margin means a loss.

How margin works

Margin = (revenue − costs) ÷ revenue × 100. For example, $10,000 in revenue minus $7,000 in costs gives a 30% margin. If costs exceed revenue, the result is a loss. Include every relevant cost in the costs field. Taxes are not added automatically.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?

Yes. There is no registration, payment, or data upload. Calculations run in your browser.

Does this include taxes?

Only if you enter them as costs. This is an estimate, not financial advice.
