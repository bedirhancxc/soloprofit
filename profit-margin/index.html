Profit Margin Calculator

Free profit margin calculator: find gross profit and margin percentage from revenue and costs.

How to calculate

Profit margin = (Revenue − Cost) ÷ Revenue × 100. For example, revenue of $10,000 and costs of $7,000 give a 30% margin. This simplified calculation does not include unentered costs or taxes.

Frequently asked questions

Are the calculations free?

Yes. There is no registration, payment, or data upload. Calculations run in your browser.

Is this financial advice?

No. These are simplified estimates to support planning. Review all relevant costs and taxes before making business decisions.
