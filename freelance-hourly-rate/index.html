Freelance Hourly Rate Calculator

Calculate your freelance hourly rate from income goals, business expenses, billable hours and working weeks.

How to calculate

Add your annual income target and operating costs, then divide by the hours you can actually invoice. Non-billable admin and marketing time should not be counted as billable hours.

Frequently asked questions

Are the calculations free?

Yes. There is no registration, payment, or data upload. Calculations run in your browser.

Is this financial advice?

No. These are simplified estimates to support planning. Review all relevant costs and taxes before making business decisions.
