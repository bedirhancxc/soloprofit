Frequently asked questions

What is a change order for freelancers?

A change order documents work requested outside an original agreed scope, including its price and timeline impact. It helps both sides decide whether to approve extra work before it starts.

Is this really free?

Yes. This first release is free to use with no login, subscription or payment information.

Where is my information stored?

Records are saved in your current browser's local storage. Clearing site data or switching devices can remove them. Use Export backup to keep a copy. Imported JSON remains on this device.

Does clicking “Approved” legally sign the change order?

No. Tracker statuses are personal labels, not verified electronic signatures. Obtain client approval through the method required by your contract and local law.

Can I use it on mobile?

Yes. The editor and tracker adapt to narrow screens. To save a PDF, use your browser's Print or Share menu and select PDF when supported.
