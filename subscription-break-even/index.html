Subscription Break-even Calculator

Calculate how many paying subscribers you need to cover fixed and per-customer costs.

How to calculate

Break-even subscribers = fixed monthly costs ÷ (monthly subscription price − variable cost per customer), rounded up. Include fees and support costs in your estimates.

Frequently asked questions

Are the calculations free?

Yes. There is no registration, payment, or data upload. Calculations run in your browser.

Is this financial advice?

No. These are simplified estimates to support planning. Review all relevant costs and taxes before making business decisions.
