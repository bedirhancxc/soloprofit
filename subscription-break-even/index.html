Subscription Break-even Calculator

Find how many paying subscribers cover your monthly costs. Results update as you type.

How to calculate

Divide monthly fixed costs by the amount left from each subscription after per-subscriber costs. Round up to a whole subscriber. Example: $1,200 ÷ ($29 − $4) = 48 subscribers. Include payment fees and support costs in your inputs.

Frequently asked questions

Are the calculations free?

Yes. There is no registration, payment, or data upload. Calculations run in your browser.

Is this financial advice?

No. These are simplified estimates to support planning. Review all relevant costs and taxes before making business decisions.
